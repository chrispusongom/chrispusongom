🌲 About Me

I'm a forestry professional with a Master of International Forestry from the University of British Columbia, specializing in forest governance, environmental policy, and Indigenous perspectives.

My work sits at the intersection of field-level forestry practice and data-driven decision making — from timber cruising and old-growth mapping in BC's Shuswap/Splatsin territory, to wildfire mitigation planning in Alberta, to community-centered conservation projects in Uganda.

I believe good forest management requires both boots on the ground and strong analytical capacity. I'm actively building skills in GIS and data science to bridge that gap.
